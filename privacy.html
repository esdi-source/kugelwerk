Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Who is responsible

Kugelwerk is made by Noah Supenkämper. Contact: open an issue on GitHub. In this policy, “we” means the maker of Kugelwerk and “you” means the person who uses Kugelwerk.

2. What Kugelwerk is

Kugelwerk is a desktop app for Windows. It runs only on your own computer. There is no Kugelwerk server, no Kugelwerk user account, and we do not receive any of your data. Everything described below happens on your computer, or directly between your computer and TikTok.

3. Data Kugelwerk uses when you connect TikTok

Connecting a TikTok account is optional. If you connect one, you log in on TikTok's own login page. Kugelwerk never sees or stores your TikTok password. With your permission, Kugelwerk receives the following data from TikTok and uses it only for the purpose listed:

  • Basic profile (scope user.info.basic): your TikTok user ID for this app (open_id), display name and profile picture. Purpose: to show which account is connected and where a draft will go.
  • Your public videos (scope video.list): video ID, title, description, creation time, duration, cover image link, share link, and the number of views, likes, comments and shares. Purpose: to see when a draft you sent has been published, and to show you how your own videos perform.
  • Profile statistics (scope user.info.stats): number of followers, followings, likes and videos. Purpose: to compare video results with the size of your account.
  • Access keys (access token and refresh token). Purpose: to talk to TikTok on your behalf without logging in again every time.

4. Videos you send to TikTok

When you click the button to send a video, Kugelwerk uploads that video file directly from your computer to TikTok (scope video.upload). The video arrives in your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is published until you publish it yourself in the TikTok app. Kugelwerk only sends a video after you have clicked the button for it.

5. Where the data is stored

  • All data stays on your computer, in the Kugelwerk program folder.
  • The access keys are encrypted with Windows' built-in data protection (DPAPI). Only your Windows user account on that computer can decrypt them.
  • We do not copy your data to any other place, server or cloud.

6. Who receives data

Only TikTok, when Kugelwerk talks to TikTok for you (login, upload, reading your videos and statistics). TikTok's own privacy policy applies to that. We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone. Kugelwerk has no advertising, no tracking and no analytics.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

  • Data is kept on your computer until you delete it.
  • When you click “Disconnect” in Kugelwerk, Kugelwerk asks TikTok to revoke the access and deletes the access keys from your computer.
  • You can also remove Kugelwerk's access at any time in the TikTok app, in the settings for connected apps and permissions.
  • Deleting the Kugelwerk program folder deletes all data Kugelwerk has stored.

8. Your rights

Under data protection law (for example the EU General Data Protection Regulation) you have the right to access, correct, delete and restrict the processing of your data, the right to data portability, and the right to object. Because Kugelwerk keeps all data on your own computer, you can exercise most of these rights yourself directly. For questions, open an issue on GitHub. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority.

9. This website

This website only shows information. It uses no cookies, no tracking and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., USA). Like any web host, the provider may process technical data such as your IP address to deliver the pages and to keep them secure. See the hosting provider's privacy policy for details.

10. Children

Kugelwerk is not meant for children. You must meet TikTok's minimum age requirements to connect a TikTok account.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
