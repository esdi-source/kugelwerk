Original physics simulation videos, made on your own computer

Kugelwerk is a desktop app for Windows. It renders short vertical videos of physics simulations: bouncing balls, races, pendulums, growing and merging shapes. Every video is computed from scratch on your computer, with your own colours, texts and sounds.

How it works

  1. Create. Pick a simulation, adjust it and render a 1080 × 1920 video at 60 fps.
  2. Connect. Connect your TikTok account once with the official TikTok login (Login Kit). Kugelwerk never sees your password.
  3. Send as draft. When you click “Send to TikTok inbox”, Kugelwerk uploads the video file to your TikTok inbox as a draft. Nothing is posted automatically.
  4. Publish in TikTok. You open the notification in the TikTok app, edit the draft if you like, and publish it yourself.
  5. Learn. Kugelwerk shows the public view, like, comment and share counts of your own videos, so you can see which simulations work best.

Your data stays with you

  • Kugelwerk runs only on your computer. There is no Kugelwerk server and no user account.
  • The TikTok access key is stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows' built-in protection.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics, no selling of data.
  • You can disconnect your TikTok account at any time, in Kugelwerk or in the TikTok app.

What Kugelwerk does not do

  • It does not post directly to your profile. You always publish in the TikTok app.
  • It does not like, follow, comment or message on your behalf.
  • It does not read other people's data.
  • It does not add logos or watermarks to your videos.
